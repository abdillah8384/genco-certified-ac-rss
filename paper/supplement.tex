\begin{abstract}
This supplement states every equation used by the code of the entry (DC model of the
robust secure set, bilevel and KKT form, the big-M baseline, the two-phase Benders
decomposition, the AC model and correction loop, GENCO's corrective-layer trajectory,
physics-trajectory recalibration with split-conformal screening, the gradient adversary
and the battery extension) and gives complete proofs of all results: the meaning of the
RSS interval (Theorem~\ref{thm:rss}), the vertex property (Lemma~\ref{lem:vertex}), validity
and finite termination of the Benders certificate (Theorems~\ref{thm:relax}--\ref{thm:phase2}),
nesting of the AC-corrected sets (Proposition~\ref{prop:nest}) and the conformal no-miss
guarantee (Theorem~\ref{thm:conf}). Square brackets name the implementing function in
\texttt{genco\_rss/}. The statements are also checked numerically by
\texttt{tests/test\_theory.py}.
\end{abstract}
